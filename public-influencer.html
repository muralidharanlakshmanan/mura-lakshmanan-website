  1. Agents in the Enterprise: Context, Control, and Reality

    Panelist · COLLIDE Data & AI Conference (Data Science Connect), Sandy Springs Performing Arts Center, Atlanta · 1 Oct 2026

    Panel on running AI agents in large enterprises, moderated by Honeywell's Sr. Director of Data Management, Governance & AI Strategy, with senior data and AI leaders from Capital One, EXP and TelevisaUnivision. We covered moving beyond prompts to enterprise-grade AI systems, what agents should remember and forget, and what it takes to scale an agentic system from prototype to production. Fellow panelist Keyur Vyas (Senior Director, Capital One) independently described the session in a LinkedIn post naming the full panel (last photo).
